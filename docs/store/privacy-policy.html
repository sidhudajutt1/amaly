Amaly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Amaly respects your privacy. This app is a free Sadaqah Jariyah project — we do not sell your data or use it for exploitative advertising.

What we collect

Location (for prayer times and Qibla), app preferences (language, theme, calculation method), and locally stored bookmarks and reading progress. All data stays on your device unless you choose to share feedback by email.

What we never do

We do not sell religious or personal data, profile users for ads, or share your worship habits with third parties.

Analytics

Amaly does not use invasive analytics. Any future diagnostics will be minimal, anonymized, and disclosed here.
